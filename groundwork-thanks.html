What happens next

  • Your property's public record and the closed sales around it are pulled and screened against your home's size, lot and age.
  • The finished PDF report is delivered to your email, typically within 1 business day.
  • If your property turns out to have fewer than three qualifying comparable sales, we'll contact you before delivering anything — you'll get a refund or the option to apply the $99 to a full appraisal.

Your $99 credit

If you decide you need a full appraisal after reading the analysis, the entire $99 is credited: a Desktop Appraisal ($449) becomes $350, and a Standard Appraisal ($699) becomes $600.
