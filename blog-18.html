May 16, 2025

San Diego, 92107 — Condominium Market Conditions Update

The San Diego 92107 (Ocean Beach / Point Loma) condominium and townhouse market was a balanced market as of the May 16, 2025 retrospective effective date. The median sale price for the applicable quarter was $840,000, up +9.2% from $769,500 the prior quarter, while the median price per square foot rose to $783.70, a +5.7% gain; the six-month median of $770,000 was +10.0% above the year-earlier figure. Inventory stood at 4.6 months across 23 active listings, supporting an anticipated marketing time of 60–90 days. Median days on market lengthened to 47 from 40, and the sale-to-list ratio held at 100.0%. Quarterly sales volume eased -6.2% (15 closings versus 16) and the trailing six-month count of 31 was -26.2% below a year earlier. A +12.4% twelve-month linear $/SF trend with +5.1% over six months indicates value movement of roughly ten to fifteen percent annualized in this thinly traded coastal condominium segment.

May 16, 2025

San Diego, 92107 — Market Conditions Update

The San Diego 92107 (Ocean Beach / Point Loma) single-family market was a balanced market as of the May 16, 2025 retrospective effective date. A +15.7% twelve-month linear $/SF trend paired with +17.6% over six months points to strong size-normalized appreciation; the divergence from the quarterly median price reflects a mix shift toward smaller homes rather than a decline in underlying value.
