What the levels mean
  • Basic Desktop — public records and comparable sales, no site visit. Same analysis as the other levels, delivered in a streamlined format. Photos come from you or from online sources, and you can request a revision if the condition or quality is misread. USPAP compliant. The budget option.
  • Desktop — a full written report with detailed comparable sales, market conditions, neighborhood maps, property history, and subject photos. No site visit.
  • Standard — a full appraisal including an in-person interior and exterior inspection, my own photographs, and a first-hand condition assessment.
With good, representative photos all three levels reach the same reliable value conclusion. The analysis is identical; what changes is the depth of the reporting and whether I have walked the property myself. Happy to talk through which one fits your situation if that would help.

Background
I regularly serve as an expert witness in litigation, trust, divorce, bankruptcy, and mediation matters, and I am often retained as a consultant to review and rebut other appraisers' work — in a fair number of those cases a clear written review resolves the issue without anyone needing to testify.
