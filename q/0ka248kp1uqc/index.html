All three options below use the same research and reach the same value conclusion; the difference is how much of the supporting work is on the page. All reports are USPAP compliant.

Your options
Basic Desktop Appraisal$299
No site visit. Public records, comparable sales, and photos you provide or that are available online. A condensed report of around 15 pages: summary, sales comparison grid with the comparables and adjustments, value conclusion, and the required certification. This is the budget option.
Desktop Appraisal$449
Everything in the Basic Desktop, plus the full written narrative: detailed property description, market conditions with charts, why each comparable was chosen and how each adjustment was derived, a location map, and photos of the subject and comparables. Typically 30–40 pages.
Recommended
Standard Appraisal$699
Everything in the Desktop, plus an in-person interior and exterior observation of the home, with my own photos and condition assessment.
Why I recommend the Standard

Not because it changes the value, but because a buyout is a decision both of you have to accept. Having me walk the home puts a firsthand record of its condition and updates in the report, which leaves less room for either side to question it later.

Photographs

For the Standard, I take my own photos at the observation. If you choose a desktop option, ideally you’d send photos of the home — wide shots from a corner of each room work better than close-ups. If that’s not practical, a general description of its condition works too, such as “remodeled kitchen and baths” or “dated but well maintained.”

Photos can be uploaded here: www.brianward.com/upload-photos
