Standard Appraisal$699

Which one fits

With good photos, all three reach the same value conclusion — the analysis is the same and the difference is how much of it is written out and whether I visit in person. The Desktop suits most estate, trust and planning needs. The Standard makes more sense when the report may be scrutinized, such as a divorce or an estate being divided among several people. The Basic Desktop is a good fit when you mainly need a credible number at the lowest cost.

Photographs

For the two desktop options, photos from you are ideal but optional — wide shots from a corner of each room, the front and rear of the house, and anything recently updated or in need of repair. If photos aren't handy, a general description such as “updated kitchen, original baths” or “dated but well maintained” works too, and I can fill in the rest from what's available online.
