Why I recommend the Desktop

For stepped-up basis, the Desktop is the report that explains how each number was reached if the basis is ever questioned. The value conclusion is the same either way.

Photographs

Ideally you'd send photos of each home, particularly any from around 2020. Wide shots from a corner of each room work better than close-ups. If you don't have photos from that period, a general description of each home's condition at the time works too — something like “original 1890s finishes, well maintained” or “kitchen and baths updated in 2015” tells me most of what I need.
